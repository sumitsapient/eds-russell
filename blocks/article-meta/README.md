# article-meta

Custom **article-meta** block. Purpose: Article byline: author names, published and updated dates, and round print / email / LinkedIn share buttons on the right.

## Authoring (Document Authoring)

Model: `standalone`

Single block table. Content: row 1: authors (one paragraph per author, optional links); row 2: 'Published: …' line; row 3: 'Updated: …' line. Lines are classified by text: 'Published…' and 'Updated…' are dates, all other lines are authors. Share buttons are generated by JS.

## Supported variations

| Variation | Option class |
| --- | --- |
| Band | `band` |

## Universal Editor fields

- `authors` (richtext): one paragraph per author, optionally linked.
- `published` (text): e.g. "Published: August 27, 2025".
- `updated` (text, optional): e.g. "Updated: October 27, 2025".
- `classes` select for options: Band.

## Share buttons

Generated by JS (no authoring): Print (`window.print()`), Email (mailto with page title and URL), LinkedIn (share-offsite URL, new window). Hidden when printing.

## Band option

Paints a full-bleed #f3f3f3 band behind the row via `border-image` outset (no stacking context, no horizontal scroll). Place the block at the top of the article body section, directly under the hero; anything that must sit on top of the band (e.g. a sticky TOC in an aside column) needs `position: relative` or `sticky`.
